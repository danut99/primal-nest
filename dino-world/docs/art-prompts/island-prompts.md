# Island artwork

## Main headquarters island

`main-island.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool using the jungle island as a style reference. Prompt: one new transparent floating headquarters island in richly painted elevated isometric dinosaur game style, landscape 1536 × 1024, a broad oval walkable top and tapering sandstone underside. Ancient expedition settlement with warm limestone terraces, a pale sandstone plaza, grass borders, cycads, ferns, a dinosaur fossil crest monument, mossy ruins, bronze details, a small pool and waterfall, and hanging vines. Five empty circular building pads across the upper-left, upper-right, middle-left, middle-right, and lower-center; functional buildings are added by the game. Small stone landing platforms on the edges. Entire silhouette visible with transparent padding. No buildings, live creatures, text, UI, sky, backdrop, or long bridges baked into the art. PNG converted to WebP preserving alpha.

## Floating causeway stone

`causeway-stone.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool using the new main island as a style and stone material reference. Prompt: one small ancient floating sandstone stepping stone matching the richly painted isometric dinosaur game style. Broad irregular limestone top, weathered grooves, a small turquoise prehistoric fossil rune, warm ochre rocky underside, tiny moss patches, fern leaves and hanging vines. Practical walkable platform, whole silhouette isolated on genuine transparent alpha, no tall monument, ground, sky, text, UI, other islands or baked exterior shadow. Painterly detail, not flat vector geometry or a geometric hexagon. Converted to a 384px WebP with alpha preserved; reused along curved animated causeways.

## Habitat islands

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using `jungle-island.webp` as a style and composition reference. The jungle image is the existing asset. Final generated PNGs were converted to WebP with alpha preserved.

Shared prompt: One standalone floating island matching the reference's richly painted colorful isometric mobile dinosaur game style, elevated three-quarter view, broad oval walkable top and tapering vertical rock underside. Entire island visible with transparent padding. Three broad natural clearings for creatures; decorations around the edges and back. No background, sky, ground plane, text, UI, buildings, or live dinosaurs. Landscape 1536 × 1024. Genuine alpha transparency and detailed silhouette.

- `fire-island.webp`: Volcanic dinosaur habitat, black basalt cliffs, ancient volcano, glowing orange lava channels, stone bridges, amber ferns and fossil skulls.
- `water-island.webp`: Aquatic dinosaur habitat, turquoise lagoon, layered waterfalls, coral and palms, blue limestone cliffs, shallow ponds, driftwood bridges and dinosaur fossils.
- `earth-island.webp`: Desert canyon dinosaur habitat, ochre sandstone mesas, sandy paths, half-buried dinosaur ribs and skulls, prehistoric cycads and a narrow oasis stream.
- `ice-island.webp`: Frozen dinosaur habitat, blue glacier cliffs, powder snow, ice arches, frozen waterfall, meltwater channels, frosted prehistoric conifers and fossils.
- `storm-island.webp`: Storm dinosaur habitat, purple slate cliffs, lavender crystals, wind-bent prehistoric conifers, silver waterfalls, glowing electric fissures and fossil skulls.
